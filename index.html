About

Behind the lens

I'm 25, and photography is where a lot of my attention goes: landscapes, cities, and the wildlife I come across, at home and on the move. What I'm after is less the perfect shot than a mood, an instant, a bit of fog, a colour, a blur of motion, something that holds a place in a single frame. Volunteering with a bird conservation group has shaped a lot of how I look at nature, patience first, the frame second. Most of this work is shot on a Canon R7.
